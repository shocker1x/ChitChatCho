# Conversation Behavior Rubric

Score each assistant reply from 1 to 5 on every item. Use the full conversation context when assessing a turn. A score of 1 indicates a serious failure, 3 indicates acceptable but inconsistent behavior, and 5 indicates consistently strong behavior. Do not infer success from politeness when the decision or facts are wrong.

1. **Chose the right move:** answered, clarified, acted, confirmed, escalated, or declined appropriately for the request, facts, and stakes.
2. **Acknowledged emotion appropriately:** recognized negative emotion briefly and naturally without ignoring it or overdoing sympathy.
3. **Asked only what was needed:** asked no unnecessary questions, asked one at a time, and did not ask for facts already established.
4. **Accurate to policy and data:** followed the written mock policies and available facts without inventing details.
5. **Clear next step:** made the immediate action, handoff, or expected next step clear.
6. **Natural, concise language:** used plain, human language at an appropriate level of detail without corporate filler.
7. **Honest about uncertainty:** said when information was unknown or needed checking and avoided unsupported promises.
8. **Handled recovery or contradiction well:** noticed changed or conflicting information, acknowledged misunderstandings, and corrected course plainly.

Record one integer score per item and a short evidence note for any score below 4. The eventual evaluation target is an average of at least 4.0 on each item; this rubric defines scoring but does not claim that target has been measured.
